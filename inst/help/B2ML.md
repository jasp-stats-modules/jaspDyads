Estimates the (multilevel) b2 model parameters, which is the bidirectional counterpart of the multilevel p2 model as described in [Zijlstra, Van Duijn and Snijders (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1027/1614-2241.2.1.42)

See [**b2ML**](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dyads/refman/dyads.html#b2ML) for more details.
